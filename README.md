# China Expat Salary Planner

Version: 0.8 Beta

Created by David Templeton

---

## Overview

China Expat Salary Planner is a free tool designed to help expatriates working in China understand their expected take-home salary.

The goal of this application is simple:

**Give expats a clearer understanding of their real financial position using available tax rules, contract timelines, and user-provided information.**

Many expats receive salary information from employers or HR departments that does not clearly explain:

- How much tax will actually be deducted
- How monthly take-home pay changes throughout the year
- How contract start dates affect cumulative taxation
- How salary changes affect future income

This application aims to provide clarity and transparency when evaluating employment offers in China.

---

## What This App Is

China Expat Salary Planner is:

- A salary estimation tool
- A financial planning aid
- A way to understand expected monthly income
- A tool to explore different salary situations
- A report generator for personal reference

---

## What This App Is Not

This application is **not**:

- Official payroll software
- HR software
- A replacement for employer calculations
- A replacement for government tax advice
- A guarantee of your final salary payment

Actual results may differ depending on:

- Government policy changes
- Employer payroll systems
- Local regulations
- Individual circumstances
- Social insurance requirements

Always verify final amounts with your employer and official sources.

## Removed Features

Previous versions included scenario import/export functionality.

This feature was removed in v0.8 to simplify the user experience and focus on the core purpose of the application:

**Enter your situation, understand your salary, and generate a clear report.**

---

## Development Philosophy

This application follows one principle:

**Help people understand their position, not replace professional payroll systems.**

The purpose is clarity and education.

It uses mathematical models based on available information and user inputs to provide estimates.

Garbage in = garbage out.

The accuracy of results depends on the accuracy of the information provided.
