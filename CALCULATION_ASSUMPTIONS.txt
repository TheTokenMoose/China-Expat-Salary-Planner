# Calculation Assumptions

**China Expat Salary Planner**  
Version 1.0.0

---

## Purpose

China Expat Salary Planner is designed to provide a practical estimate of expected take-home income for expatriates working in China.

The application is intended to improve understanding of employment offers and contract terms. It is **not** a payroll system, tax filing application, or official calculator.

The results produced by this application should be considered estimates based on the information entered by the user and the calculation assumptions described below.

---

## General Assumptions

The calculator assumes that:

- All information entered by the user is accurate.
- Salary figures are entered in Chinese Renminbi (RMB).
- The employment contract is legal and follows standard Chinese payroll practices.
- Income is received monthly unless otherwise specified.
- The user has entered all relevant salary adjustments, bonuses, previous income, and benefits.

Incorrect or incomplete information will produce inaccurate estimates.

---

## Tax Calculation

The calculator estimates Individual Income Tax (IIT) using publicly available Chinese tax rules.

**For China tax residents:**

- Income is calculated using cumulative year-to-date taxable income.
- The standard monthly deduction of ¥5,000 is applied.
- Tax already paid earlier in the year is taken into account when previous income has been entered.
- Tax brackets are applied according to cumulative taxable income.

**For China non-residents:**

- Tax is estimated using the simplified monthly calculation method.
- This is intended as a practical estimate and may not cover every individual circumstance.

---

## Previous Income

If your contract begins after January, previous income and tax paid earlier in the calendar year may affect your tax position.

The calculator allows previous income to be entered so that cumulative tax calculations can begin from a more realistic position.

If no previous income is entered, the calculator assumes:

- No taxable income has been earned earlier in the calendar year.
- No tax has already been paid.

---

## Salary Adjustments

Salary changes entered into the planner affect future months only.

Two adjustment types are supported:

- **Ongoing adjustment** – replaces the monthly salary from the selected date onwards.
- **One-time adjustment** – adds a single payment during the selected month without changing future salary.

---

## Bonus Payments

Bonus payments may be treated differently from normal salary under Chinese tax regulations.

The calculator currently treats bonuses as normal taxable income in the month they are paid. This is a practical estimate.

Actual employer payroll practices may differ.

---

## Benefits

Benefits such as:

- Housing
- Transport
- Flights
- Allowances
- Other package items

may be included in the overall compensation package.

In this version, benefits are treated as **untaxed additions** to take-home pay.

The calculator does not automatically determine the tax treatment of benefits under Chinese regulations.

---

## Social Insurance

The calculator can estimate employee social insurance deductions using user-provided percentages.

Please note:

- Social insurance rules differ by city.
- Contribution bases and caps change periodically.
- Employer contribution rates are not included.
- Some expatriates may be exempt depending on nationality, bilateral agreements, employer policies, or local regulations.

For these reasons, social insurance calculations should be regarded as estimates.

---

## Tax Residency

Selecting the correct tax residency status is important.

The calculator provides:

- China Tax Resident
- China Non-Resident
- Resident Estimate (for users who are unsure)

Users are responsible for selecting the option that best reflects their own circumstances.

---

## Contract Period

The planner calculates salary only within the contract period entered by the user.

Changes outside that period are ignored.

---

## What the Calculator Does Not Include

To keep the application simple, reliable, and easy to use, the following are intentionally not included:

- Employer payroll systems
- Company-specific payroll policies
- City-specific social insurance contribution bases and caps
- Automatic updates for changing tax regulations
- Special individual tax deductions
- Foreign employee tax exemptions
- Multi-contract optimisation
- Tax filing assistance
- Government reporting

---

## Accuracy

Every effort has been made to produce realistic estimates based on publicly available information and commonly used calculation methods.

However, actual salary payments may differ because of:

- Employer payroll practices
- Local regulations
- Government policy changes
- Individual tax circumstances
- Social insurance requirements
- Benefit treatment
- Contract-specific arrangements

Always confirm final salary calculations with your employer, HR department, payroll provider, or an appropriately qualified tax professional.

---

## Project Philosophy

China Expat Salary Planner was created to answer one simple question:

**"What will I probably take home each month if I accept this job?"**

The goal is clarity rather than complexity.

Instead of attempting to replicate professional payroll software, this application focuses on providing understandable, transparent estimates that help users make better-informed employment decisions.
